# Nurava HubStores Supplier Agreement

**Supplier product sourcing and fulfilment terms**

This Agreement is made on ____________________ between:

- **Platform:** ____________________, a ____________________ registered in ____________________, registration number ____________________, of ____________________ ("Platform"); and
- **Supplier:** ____________________, a ____________________ registered in ____________________, registration number ____________________, of ____________________ ("Supplier").

Each is a "Party" and together they are the "Parties".

## 1. Purpose and relationship

The Platform operates its own online store and may list products sourced from the Supplier. The Platform is the seller and customer-facing store operator for orders it accepts. The Supplier supplies the agreed products and may fulfil orders by packing and shipping them directly to customers or through an agreed delivery partner. The Supplier is an independent contractor, not the Platform's agent, employee, partner, or joint venturer. Neither Party may bind the other.

## 2. Products and listing approval

The Supplier will provide accurate product descriptions, specifications, images it is authorized to license, stock status, supplier references, costs, warranty details, restrictions, and delivery estimates. The Platform controls retail prices, storefront presentation, and whether a listing is published. No product is approved until both Parties record it in **Schedule A** or approve it in writing. The Supplier must promptly report any safety concern, recall, listing error, restriction, or material change. The Platform may pause or remove a listing to protect customers, comply with law, or manage operational risk.

## 3. Orders and fulfilment

The Platform sends the Supplier the order and only the information reasonably needed to fulfil it. The Supplier will acknowledge an order within ____________________, confirm stock, and dispatch within the agreed handling time in Schedule A. It will use the agreed carrier, packaging, tracking, and delivery service; provide tracking promptly; and not substitute, split, cancel, or charge extra for an order without the Platform's written approval. The Supplier must not include unsolicited marketing or direct customers to bypass the Platform. The Supplier will promptly tell the Platform about stockouts, delays, failed delivery, damage, or other fulfilment issues and cooperate on a remedy.

## 4. Price, settlement, and records

For each fulfilled item, the Platform owes the Supplier the agreed supplier cost and approved delivery charges shown in Schedule A, subject to documented adjustments for cancellations, returns, refunds, chargebacks, shortages, or defects. The Parties will reconcile orders ____________________ and settle undisputed amounts within **____________________ days** after ____________________ using ____________________. The Supplier must issue any required invoices and keep accurate order, dispatch, delivery, and adjustment records for ____________________. The Supplier may not change a cost or fee without advance written agreement. Taxes, withholding, and applicable invoices will be handled as required by law and as specified in Schedule A.

## 5. Product quality, compliance, and warranties

The Supplier warrants that products supplied are authentic, safe, new unless expressly described otherwise, fit the stated specifications, free from undisclosed liens, and compliant with applicable product, labelling, import, safety, and intellectual-property requirements. The Supplier will maintain all permits and approvals required for its supply activities. It will provide product instructions, warnings, certificates, and warranty terms before listing. The Supplier must not supply counterfeit, recalled, prohibited, or materially misdescribed goods. Any Supplier or manufacturer warranty must be stated in Schedule A; it does not remove customer rights or the Platform's obligations to customers.

## 6. Delivery, cancellation, returns, and customer issues

The Supplier is responsible for careful packing, timely dispatch, and the agreed delivery handoff. Risk of loss and responsibility for a product that is lost, damaged, incorrect, faulty, or not as described remains with the Supplier until ____________________, subject to mandatory law. The Platform manages customer communication and decides the customer remedy in accordance with applicable law and its published store terms. The Supplier will promptly provide return instructions and cooperate with cancellation, replacement, repair, refund, warranty, and delivery claims. The Parties will allocate return and redelivery costs in Schedule A; where an issue is caused by the Supplier's breach, error, defect, or inadequate packing, the Supplier bears the reasonable resulting costs. The Supplier will reimburse the Platform for the agreed supplier cost and related charges for affected items where the Platform refunds a customer for a Supplier-caused issue.

## 7. Customer information and security

The Supplier may use customer personal data only on the Platform's documented instructions and only to fulfil the relevant order, arrange delivery, resolve the order issue, or meet a legal obligation. The Supplier must keep it confidential; limit access to authorized personnel bound by confidentiality; apply appropriate technical and organizational safeguards; use no subcontractor or onward recipient without written approval and equivalent written protections; make no marketing use or independent contact; and promptly notify the Platform of a suspected breach or unauthorized disclosure. The Supplier will assist with data-subject requests, security investigations, and legally required assessments. At the Platform's choice, the Supplier will return or securely delete the data when fulfilment and any legally required retention end, and confirm deletion on request. The Parties will complete **Schedule B** before any customer data is shared. Each Party will comply with applicable data-protection law and document any required controller-processor terms.

## 8. Confidentiality and intellectual property

Each Party will protect the other Party's non-public commercial, technical, customer, and operational information and use it only to perform this Agreement. The Supplier grants the Platform a non-exclusive, royalty-free licence during the listing period to use Supplier-provided product names, descriptions, and approved images to market and sell the products through the Platform's store and related order communications. The Supplier confirms it has the rights needed to grant that licence. Each Party retains ownership of its pre-existing materials. Confidentiality does not restrict information that is public without breach, already lawfully known, independently developed, or required to be disclosed by law (with prior notice where lawful).

## 9. Liability and indemnity

Each Party is responsible for its own breach, negligence, and legal compliance. The Supplier will indemnify the Platform against third-party claims, losses, reasonable legal costs, product recall costs, and customer remedies to the extent arising from the Supplier's defective, unsafe, counterfeit, unlawful, or misdescribed products; infringement of third-party rights by Supplier materials; Supplier's fulfilment breach; or Supplier's unlawful use or disclosure of customer data. The Platform will promptly notify the Supplier and reasonably cooperate in the defence. Neither Party excludes liability that cannot legally be excluded. Any other liability cap or exclusions must be written here before signing: ____________________.

## 10. Term, suspension, and ending the Agreement

This Agreement starts on the Effective Date and continues until ended. Either Party may end it on **____________________ days'** written notice. A Party may end it immediately for an uncured material breach after **____________________ days'** notice, insolvency, unlawful conduct, serious product-safety risk, or a material data-security incident. The Platform may immediately pause listings or new orders where reasonably needed to protect customers or comply with law. On termination, the Parties will complete, cancel, or otherwise resolve open orders; reconcile undisputed amounts; stop use of the other Party's marks and materials; and return or delete customer information as required by clause 7. Clauses intended to continue, including confidentiality, data handling, payment reconciliation, liability, and dispute resolution, survive termination.

## 11. General terms and disputes

This Agreement and its schedules are the entire agreement for the products covered and replace prior discussions on that subject. Changes must be in writing and approved by both Parties. Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other's written consent, except to a successor to substantially all of its relevant business that accepts these terms. If one term is unenforceable, the remaining terms continue. Notices must be sent to the addresses and emails in the signature block. The Parties will first attempt in good faith to resolve a dispute through their designated representatives within **____________________ days**, then refer it to the courts of ____________________, unless they complete this alternative dispute clause: ____________________. This Agreement is governed by the laws of ____________________.
